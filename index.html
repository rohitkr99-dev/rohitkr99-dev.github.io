About Me

A project leader built
for global complexity

I'm a Senior Project Manager specializing in EPC export fabrication — the discipline of managing piping systems manufactured in Thailand and deployed across the world's most demanding energy infrastructure projects.

My career began as a mechanical engineer at DEE Development Engineers Ltd., where I developed deep expertise in piping design, stress analysis, and fabrication standards. Over 13 years I've evolved into a full-spectrum project leader managing everything from client contracts and engineering coordination to procurement strategies and international shipping logistics.

What distinguishes me is the rare combination of hands-on technical depth and executive-level project oversight — I can read isometric drawings in the morning and present cost forecasts to C-suite stakeholders in the afternoon.
